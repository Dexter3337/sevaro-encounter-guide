01 / Problem statement

Hospital rules change.
The encounter keeps moving.

How can a provider find and apply the correct local workflow without repeatedly leaving the caselog?

CONTEXT

Different hospitals

Ordering responsibility, video location, drug preparation and completion rules vary.

TIME

Different coverage

Staffed windows, overnight schedules and hospital timezones change the relevant action.

TRUST

Different source quality

Missing, stale or conflicting instructions should remain visible rather than turn into confident answers.

Design problem derived from the supplied challenge and synthetic hospital guides; no patient-outcome claim.

06 / Completion workflow

Confirmation must be explicit.

REQUIREMENT UNMET

Block and explain

Identify the charge or provider-confirmation requirement, cite its source and link to the field to fix.

REQUIREMENT RECORDED

Reevaluate and complete

Evaluate the latest revision. Record mock completion only after supported gates are satisfied.

REQUESTComplete
→
CHECKSource + explicit field
→
RESULTFix blocker / mock success

Unverifiable requirements show uncertainty and do not create invented hard stops. No EHR note signature or charge posting occurs.
